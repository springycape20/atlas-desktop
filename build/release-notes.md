ATLAS 0.6.14 makes Settings easier to find your way around.

- **Settings lists its sections on the left:** Provider accounts, Models, Assistant, Voice, Microphone, Permissions, Startup, App updates, and Tools and data. One section shows at a time, and Settings reopens where you left off. You can also move between sections with the arrow keys.
- **Search any setting.** Type words like "wake", "timeout" or "voice" to see just the matching settings, even across sections. Press Escape to clear the search.
- **The new Tools and data section** holds Installed plugins and tools, Import chats from Codex or Claude, and Show log file.
- **Settings tells you about unsaved changes,** and warns you if you close it without saving.
- **In narrow windows,** the sections become a row you can scroll.
- ATLAS is now released under the MIT License.

Install over your existing version to preserve conversations and settings. Packaged installations can use **Settings > App updates > Check for updates**, then download and restart to install. Windows x64. Close older ATLAS instances before updating. Computer control uses the separate Windows-MCP integration, not another chat application's private runtime.
