Local data controls

No server, tracking endpoint, account list or automatic uploads. This app retains eligible reports for the latest twelve calendar months and removes expired reports when opened or used. Up to 5,000 reports are retained.

Reports and their summaries are stored on this computer under its account permissions; they are not encrypted by this app. Sharing channels can identify a sender. Do not join receipts to contact lists or publish small groups. Review originals, messages, exports and backups separately when deleting data.

Nothing is transmitted by this admin app.
